# The content model, judged against Spanish transparency law

**Date:** 2026-08-24 · **Author:** [ejecutor] · **Purpose:** re-decide D-026 against the domain
rather than against surface cost, per [andres]'s ruling that cost is not the criterion.

Per I-001 this research expires between turns. Everything below states what was read, where, and
when. Two items are marked NOT SOURCE-VERIFIED and carry the command that would verify them.

## 1 · The specification: Ley 19/2013, arts. 6-8

**Source:** BOE consolidated text, `https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2013-12887`,
read 2026-08-24. The page states its last consolidated update as **09/07/2022**.

The fields the law *names* are in bold, because a field the law names is a field a table must
expose, and a taxonomy term cannot carry a number.

### Art. 6.1 — Información institucional y organizativa

> "…incluirán un **organigrama actualizado** que identifique a **los responsables de los diferentes
> órganos** y su **perfil y trayectoria profesional**."

### Art. 6.2 — Planificación

> "…publicarán los **planes y programas anuales y plurianuales**… Su **grado de cumplimiento y
> resultados** deberán ser objeto de evaluación y publicación periódica junto con los
> **indicadores de medida y valoración**."

### Art. 6 bis — Registro de actividades de tratamiento
Inventory of processing activities under the data-protection organic law. A document.

### Art. 7 — Información de relevancia jurídica
Directrices e instrucciones; anteproyectos de ley; proyectos de reglamento; memorias e informes de
los expedientes normativos; documentos sometidos a información pública. **All are files with a date
and a legal reference.** The paradigm "document with metadata" category.

### Art. 8.1 — Información económica, presupuestaria y estadística

**a) Contratos.**
> "Todos los contratos, con indicación del **objeto**, **duración**, el **importe de la
> licitación** y de **adjudicación**, el **procedimiento** utilizado para su celebración, los
> **instrumentos a través de los que, en su caso, se ha publicitado**, el **número de licitadores
> participantes** en el procedimiento y la **identidad del adjudicatario**, así como las
> **modificaciones** del contrato…"

and, critically:

> "Asimismo, se publicarán **datos estadísticos sobre el porcentaje en volumen presupuestario de
> contratos adjudicados a través de cada uno de los procedimientos**"

> "La publicación de la información relativa a los **contratos menores podrá realizarse
> trimestralmente**."

**b) Convenios.**
> "La relación de los convenios suscritos, con mención de las **partes firmantes**, su **objeto**,
> **plazo de duración**, **modificaciones realizadas**, **obligados a la realización de las
> prestaciones** y, en su caso, las **obligaciones económicas convenidas**…"

**c) Subvenciones.**
> "Las subvenciones y ayudas públicas concedidas con indicación de su **importe**, **objetivo o
> finalidad** y **beneficiarios**."

**d) Presupuestos.**
> "Los presupuestos, con **descripción de las principales partidas presupuestarias** e información
> actualizada y comprensible sobre su **estado de ejecución**…"

**e) Cuentas anuales** y los informes de auditoría y de fiscalización por órganos de control externo.

**f) Retribuciones.**
> "Las **retribuciones percibidas anualmente** por los **altos cargos y máximos responsables**…
> Igualmente, se harán públicas las **indemnizaciones percibidas**, en su caso, con ocasión del
> abandono del cargo."

**g) Compatibilidades.** Resoluciones de autorización o reconocimiento de compatibilidad.

**h) Declaraciones de bienes — explicitly municipal.**
> "Las **declaraciones anuales de bienes y actividades de los representantes locales**, en los
> términos previstos en la Ley 7/1985, de 2 de abril, Reguladora de las Bases del Régimen Local…"

**Art. 8.2** extends a) and b) to contracts and convenios signed with a Public Administration by
the art. 3 subjects — **this is the article that makes the template relevant to the "accountable
foundation" half of Ágora's audience**, not only to municipalities.

**Art. 8.3** — "…publicarán la **relación de los bienes inmuebles** que sean de su propiedad o sobre
los que ostenten algún derecho real." Tabular, not narrative.

### What is NOT in the verified text

**Oferta de empleo público / relación de puestos de trabajo.** Commonly listed among the
obligations and published by `transparencia.gob.es`, but **not** in the verified text of arts. 6, 7
or 8 read today — plausibly autonomic law or the EBEP. **Recorded as unresolved.** It does not
change the model: a convocatoria is a set of bases with a deadline and a resolution, i.e. a
document.

## 2 · The open-data obligation is a *different* law, and it binds local entities

The Dataset requirement does not come from Ley 19/2013 at all. It comes from **Ley 37/2007 de
reutilización de la información del sector público** (transposing the Open Data Directive
(UE) 2019/1024) and, for the high-value list, from **Reglamento de Ejecución (UE) 2023/138**, which
as a Regulation applies directly without transposition.

⚠️ **NOT SOURCE-VERIFIED IN THIS TURN.** Search results (BOE `DOUE-L-2023-80077`; EUR-Lex
`32023R0138`) consistently give six thematic categories — *geoespacial · observación de la Tierra y
medio ambiente · meteorología · estadística · sociedades y propiedad de sociedades · movilidad* —
with a 16-month availability deadline from 2023-01-20 and a machine-readable/API requirement. This
is snippet-level evidence only. Verify with:

    curl -sL "https://www.boe.es/doue/2023/019/L00043-00075.pdf" -o hvd.pdf   # then read Anexo

⚠️ **ALSO NOT VERIFIED:** the NTI-RISP sector taxonomy that `datos.gob.es` federates on. If the
Dataset type's theme vocabulary is aligned to it, the template's datasets become harvestable by the
national catalogue — a genuine product feature. Verification is a task criterion, not a decision.

## 3 · What real Spanish portals actually do

**`transparencia.gob.es` — Publicidad Activa** (read 2026-08-24). Six top-level groupings:
`Organización y Empleo Público` · `Altos Cargos` · `Planificación y Estadística` · `Normativa y
Otras Disposiciones` · **`Contratos, Convenios y Subvenciones`** · `Información
Económico-Presupuestaria`. The state portal groups the three financial regimes under one heading
**but keeps them as three distinct sub-sections with their own listings** — it does not merge them
into one record type.

**Zaragoza, `www.zaragoza.es/sede/portal/transparencia/`** (read 2026-08-24, after a 301 from
`gobiernoabierto.zaragoza.es`). Observed: *Declaraciones de bienes y actividades*; **four separate
retribuciones listings** (personal eventual, empleados altamente cualificados, personal directivo,
miembros de la corporación); *Ejecución presupuestaria* presented as a **visualisation tool**, not a
document list; *Plan estratégico de subvenciones* as a visualisation; a geoportal for open data; an
organigram visualisation. The landing page did not expose the column sets of the contratos and
convenios listings — recorded as not observed rather than inferred.

**Alcobendas, `transparencia.alcobendas.org`** — the fetch failed with a TLS SAN mismatch: the host
is served by a certificate valid for `*.gobierto.es`. That failure is itself the finding: a mid-size
Spanish municipality's transparency portal **is** Gobierto.

**Gobierto (`gobierto.es`, Populate)** — the incumbent in this market. Its transparency offering
models, by its own description: **budgets, contracts, planning documents, senior officials and their
agendas, and open datasets.** Clients named include Barcelona, Madrid and Terrassa. A separate
`Gobierto Contratación` product exists for procurement. Open-source status and stack are **not
stated on the page** — do not assert either.

**The shape this reveals:** budgets are rendered as *visualisations over data*, never as one page
per budget line. Contracts are a first-class entity with their own product. Senior officials are an
entity with structured attributes. Three independent confirmations of the same model shape, from a
vendor with real municipal customers.

**LocalGov Drupal** (docs.localgovdrupal.org, read 2026-08-24) models `Services`, `Directories` and
news. **It does not model contracts, convenios, subvenciones, budgets or senior-official
remuneration.** UK local government publishes under a different regime and LocalGov's centre of
gravity is service delivery, not accountability.

**Conclusion: nothing to be consistent with.** There is no published Drupal content model for
Spanish transparency obligations. Ágora is not diverging from a convention; there is none. This is
a finding in the project's favour and belongs in the marketplace pitch.

## 5 · The autonomic layer: material in kind, not in shape

Snippet-level only (searches, 2026-08-24); no autonomic statute was read at source this turn.

- **Andalucía, Ley 1/2014** — adds publication of **actas de plenos** of local entities.
- **Cataluña, Ley 19/2014** — extended obligations to privately financed entities; precursor to the
  state's art. 8.2 direction.
- Local entities must provide internet access to plenary sessions, by streaming or recording.

**Assessment: noise for the model, signal for the demo content.** Every observed autonomic addition
is *a document* or *a media file*. None introduces a field family with numbers a table must sort.
The model does not change; the `document type` vocabulary needs `acta de pleno`, and unit 003 needs
to know a municipality in Andalucía will use it heavily. **Do not attempt seventeen autonomic
variants in a template** — a template that tries to be all of them is one no municipality
recognises.

## 6 · The rule this evidence yields

One test, applied to every category, explains every inclusion and every exclusion:

> **A category earns its own node type when the law names three or more fields for it that are
> neither prose nor a file, and at least one of them is a number or a counterparty that a table
> must sort or filter on. Otherwise it is a Document with a type, or it is a Dataset.**

| Category | Fields the law names | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Contratos (8.1.a) | 9 named, 3 numeric, 1 enumerated, 1 counterparty — **plus a legally required derived statistic** | **own type** |
| Convenios (8.1.b) | 6 named, 1 numeric, counterparties, a date range | **own type** |
| Subvenciones (8.1.c) | 3 named: importe, finalidad, beneficiario | **own type** (clears by exactly one) |
| Altos cargos (6.1 + 8.1.f) | cargo, órgano, trayectoria, **retribución anual**, **indemnización** | **own type** |
| Datasets (Ley 37/2007, Rgto. 2023/138) | distribución, formato, licencia, frecuencia, cobertura | **own type** |
| Presupuestos (8.1.d) | 2 numbers per row, but **the row is not the unit of publication** | **Dataset + Document** |
| Cuentas, informes, normativa, planes, compatibilidades, declaraciones, convocatorias, actas | file + date + type (+ at most one deadline) | **Document** |

### Why "budget line" is the wrong shape

1. A Spanish municipal budget at *partida* granularity runs to hundreds or thousands of rows. One
   node per row means a revision table, a URL, an edit form, a search-index entry and an access
   check **per budget row**.
2. The law asks for the "**principales** partidas" plus the "**estado de ejecución**" — an aggregate
   view, not a page per line.
3. A budget row has no standalone identity a citizen would link to. Nobody bookmarks
   `/partida/2026-920-22699`.
4. Every real portal observed renders budgets as a visualisation over a data table.
5. **It makes the accessibility story better, not worse.** ROADMAP 003 point 4 already demands
   "lightweight visualisation **+ accessible table as the source of truth**". If the source of truth
   is a Dataset with a rendered `<table>`, the table has one `<caption>`, real `<th scope>` and a
   coherent row set. If it is 800 nodes rendered through a View, the table is a listing of entity
   teasers wearing a table costume.

**Budget = a Document (the approved budget, the execution reports, the cuentas anuales) + a Dataset
(the machine-readable execution table, which renders as the accessible table and feeds any chart).**

### Why "Document with optional structured fields" collapses

Follow it through: `Document` acquires `importe de licitación`, `importe de adjudicación`,
`procedimiento`, `nº de licitadores`, `adjudicatario`, `obligaciones económicas`, `beneficiario`.
`Document` is then a **union type**, and it fails on both hard constraints:

- **Accessibility.** One listing whose columns are the union of every regime produces rows where a
  third of the cells are structurally empty. An empty `<td>` that is empty *by design* is
  indistinguishable, to a screen-reader user, from missing data. That is a worse table.
- **Editorial.** The clerk adding a subvención sees `número de licitadores` on the form. In a
  template whose thesis is "a municipality can actually use this to meet its legal obligations",
  that is the moment it stops being usable.

The refutation is not that it is expensive. It is that it is wrong.
